Understanding disease
Through the lens of gene regulation.

We seek to uncover how gene-environment interactions shape the development and progression of cardiometabolic diseases through innovative experimental and computational approaches spanning physiology, cell biology, genetics, and genomics. Our goal is to transform our discoveries into strategies for the prevention, prediction, and treatment of cardiometabolic diseases.

Our science

Research

Connecting molecular mechanisms with whole-organism physiology.

01

Genes, environment & cardiometabolic disease

How do genetic variants, diet, aging, and inflammation interact to influence metabolic liver disease? We investigate the regulatory mechanisms underlying differences in disease susceptibility.

02

Communication between organs

We study how liver-derived signals affect other organs, including the heart, during cardiometabolic stress.

03

Adaptation to metabolic stress

Comparative biology offers new perspectives on metabolic resilience. We investigate genetic and epigenetic adaptations in nontraditional model organisms.
